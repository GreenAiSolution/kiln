What the ceiling means
  This core can retire about 3.3 4-wide FMAs per cycle.
  KILN's matmul reaches 98% of that with no packing pass and no assembler.

  Apple's Accelerate beats this by roughly an order of magnitude,
  and not by writing better NEON: it dispatches to AMX, an on-die
  matrix coprocessor whose instruction encoding Apple does not
  publish. There is no sequence of documented ARM instructions that
  reaches it. That is the real ceiling on 'from scratch' here, and
  it is worth naming rather than hiding.
